Seus dados de navegação

Com sua permissão, estes sites usam Google Analytics para medir visitas, projetos acessados, dispositivo, origem do acesso e localização aproximada. Não solicitamos localização precisa nem usamos anúncios ou personalização publicitária.

Your browsing data

With your permission, these sites use Google Analytics to measure visits, projects, devices, traffic sources and approximate location. We do not request precise location or use advertising features.

We send only the hostname and public language pages, excluding query parameters, fragments, form content, names, email addresses and typed text. Google processes connection information, including IP addresses to derive an approximate region.

Collection starts only after “Allow”. Declining does not affect the projects. The preference and analytics cookies may last up to six months and are shared across raphaelrocha.com and its subdomains. To withdraw consent, select “Privacy and cookies” at the bottom of the page, then “Decline”. We respect your browser’s Do Not Track signal.

Reports are private. Hosting and essential project services may process data needed to function independently of these analytics. This choice covers Raphael Rocha’s personal analytics, not external services opened through links.
